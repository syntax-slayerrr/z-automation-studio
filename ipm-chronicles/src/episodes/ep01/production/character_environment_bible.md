# Character & Environment Design Bible — THE IPM CHRONICLES: Episode 01

## 1. Visual Style Direction (Part 4 Compliance)
- **Style**: Realistic-Stylized 3D Animation (Cinematography-driven documentary reconstruction, motivated natural/practical lighting, shared filmic grade and subtle grain).
- **Period Differentiation Rule**: Never reuse a single identical room set across all eras. Each historical period (`1919 Yogyakarta`, `1926 Malang/Surakarta`, `1950 Wajo`, `1955 Empty Hall`, `1958 Garut`) features distinct architectural materials, lighting temperatures, and wardrobe silhouettes.

## 2. Character Reference Sheets (Deliverable #3)

### Character Group A — Pelajar & Guru Madrasah Mu'allimin (Yogyakarta, 1919)
- **Era & Context**: Early 20th-century Yogyakarta (`Siswo Projo`, 1919).
- **Wardrobe & Silhouette**:
  - **Students**: Loose ivory/off-white cotton tunics (*baju kurung / kemeja putih lengan panjang tanpa kerah kaku*), traditional dark woven *sarung* or dark earth-tone trousers, traditional Javanese *blangkon* (batik sogan brown) or early black velvet *peci/kopiah*.
  - **Teacher (Ustadz/Pamog)**: Dignified posture, cream-white long coat/jas tutup over batik sarung, dark peci/blangkon, holding a leather-bound book.
- **Props**: Fountain pens/dip pens, thick cream-paper ledgers, classical kitab and early school notebooks.
- **Camera Framing**: Medium tracking shots (`Shot 05`) and desk-level medium close-ups focusing on hands, notebooks, and shoulders (`Shot 06`).

### Character Group B — Kelompok Diskusi Pelajar GKPM (Malang & Surakarta, 1926)
- **Era & Context**: Mid-1920s Dutch East Indies urban student movement (`Gabungan Keluarga Pelajar Muhammadiyah`, 1926).
- **Wardrobe & Silhouette**:
  - Distinct from 1919: Structured 1920s collared shirts in muted sepia/cream, dark vests or light cotton jackets, black *peci nasional* (more prevalent by the mid-1920s), neat trousers.
- **Props**: Typewritten circulars, fountain pens, folded organizational statutes on a dark teak table.
- **Camera Framing**: 3/4 study-circle medium shot (`Shot 09`) with warm kerosene/incandescent sidelight.

### Character Group C — Pelajar Rintisan Lokal Wajo (Sulawesi Selatan, 1950)
- **Era & Context**: Early post-independence Eastern Indonesia (`Wajo, Sulawesi Selatan`, 1950).
- **Wardrobe & Silhouette**:
  - Simple 1950s post-independence attire: rolled-sleeve white/khaki shirts, black *songkok/peci*, modest woven sarung or cotton trousers, reflecting regional sincerity and resourcefulness.
- **Props**: Handwritten meeting minutes, wooden benches, simple hurricane lamp on a timber table.
- **Camera Framing**: Intimate medium-wide gathering shot (`Shot 11`) shifting into subtle contemplative body language.

### Character Group D — Peserta Konferensi Pemuda Muhammadiyah (Garut, 1958)
- **Era & Context**: Formal national youth conference in West Java (`Konferensi Pemuda Muhammadiyah Garut`, 1958).
- **Wardrobe & Silhouette**:
  - Generic historical delegates (no specific real-person facial claims): 1950s formal safari jackets, dark wool/cotton blazers (charcoal, olive-brown, navy) over white collared shirts, black *peci nasional*, fountain pens, conference folders.
- **Props**: Conference agenda folders, wooden gavel/podium silhouette, brass-rimmed spectacles, structured notes.
- **Camera Framing**: Medium-wide hall perspective (`Shot 17`) with foreground backs/shoulders and rim-lit delegates discussing systematically.

## 3. Environment Reference Sheets (Deliverable #4)

### Environment 01 — Ruang Arsip & Siluet Pertemuan (Scene 01: `1961`)
- **Architecture & Materials**: Aged dark teak desk (`#1e1610`), brass inkwell, heavy archival ledger stamped `1961`, slatted wooden window blinds casting sharp volumetric light shafts across a dark charcoal-amber room.
- **Lighting**: High-contrast chiaroscuro (`3200K` warm amber shaft cutting through `#090d16` shadow).

### Environment 02 — Lingkungan Yogyakarta & Madrasah Mu'allimin (Scene 02: `1919`)
- **Architecture & Materials**:
  - **Exterior (`Shot 04`)**: Early 20th-century Yogyakarta neighborhood (Kauman/Ketanggungan vibe) — traditional Javanese *kampung* roofs (*joglo/limasan* terracotta tiles), whitewashed plaster walls, dusty lane, warm morning haze.
  - **Interior (`Shot 05–06`)**: Open-air veranda and timber classroom with dark wooden shutters, chalkboard, long wooden benches, and warm morning sunlight (`4200K`).

### Environment 03 — Ruang Diskusi Pelajar Era 1926 (Scene 03: `1926 Malang / Surakarta`)
- **Architecture & Materials**: Colonial-era brick-and-teak study room, arched window casements, dark green/ochre wall wainscoting, round study table covered with printed bulletins and notes.
- **Lighting**: Late-afternoon golden-sepia cross-light (`3500K`).

### Environment 04 — Balai Pertemuan Sederhana Wajo (Scene 04: `1950 Sulawesi Selatan`)
- **Architecture & Materials**: Elevated timber house/meeting hall (*rumah panggung kayu*), slatted wooden walls letting in humid tropical dusk light, simple wooden table and benches, warm lantern glow.
- **Lighting**: Split warm lantern glow (`2900K`) vs cool twilight outside (`5500K`), visually echoing internal dynamics and unfinished questions.

### Environment 05 — Ruang Rapat Kosong & Pintu Terbuka (Scene 05: `The Central Question`)
- **Architecture & Materials**: Quiet, unoccupied wooden meeting hall. Rows of empty wooden chairs facing a solitary table where an unfinished organizational document rests.
- **Lighting**: Solitary diagonal sunbeam filled with slow-drifting dust motes (`Shot 13–14`), gradually dimming into deep shadow before a warm rim-light spills through the opening doorway (`Shot 15`).

### Environment 06 — Lanskap Pegunungan & Aula Konferensi Garut (Scene 06: `1958 Garut`)
- **Architecture & Materials**:
  - **Exterior (`Shot 16`)**: Misty Priangan highland silhouettes (Mount Guntur / Garut basin), tiled town roofs, cool morning mountain mist breaking into warm sunlight.
  - **Interior (`Shot 17`)**: Larger 1950s conference hall with structured rows of desks, wooden podium, tall windows with crisp daylight shafts, and organized conference dossiers.
