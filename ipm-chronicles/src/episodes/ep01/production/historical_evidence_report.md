## 2. Historical Guardrails Enforced in Production

1. **No Fictional Dialogue**: Zero spoken character lines exist in Episode 01. Only the documentary narrator speaks.
2. **Clear Reconstruction Labeling**: Every dramatized historical scene (`Shot 05`, `Shot 06`, `Shot 09`, `Shot 11`, `Shot 17`) carries a subtle, dignified on-screen `REKONSTRUKSI` indicator so viewers never mistake 3D historical reconstruction for literal archival photography.
3. **Institutional Distinction**: *Siswo Projo* (1919), *GKPM* (1926), and the local *Wajo* initiative (1950) are visually and narratively presented as distinct precursor efforts (`BANYAK IKHTIAR. SATU KEBUTUHAN.`), never conflated as identical to the national IPM founded in 1961.
4. **Generic Conference Participants**: In `Shot 17` (Konferensi Pemuda Muhammadiyah Garut 1958), characters are depicted as respectful period silhouettes and medium-shot generic delegates in 1950s formal attire, avoiding unverified facial claims of specific historical figures.
