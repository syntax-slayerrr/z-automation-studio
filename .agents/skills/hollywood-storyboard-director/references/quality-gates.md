# Storyboard Quality Gates

## Hard-fail checks

Do not label a board production-ready if any of these remain unresolved:

1. **Unclear action:** the artist or animator cannot tell what happens in the shot.
2. **Missing dramatic purpose:** a shot has no identifiable story or emotional function.
3. **Broken geography:** positions, screen direction, or eyelines conflict without a motivated reset.
4. **Unaccounted runtime:** shot durations materially exceed or fall short of the target.
5. **Historical fabrication:** invented details are presented as documented fact.
6. **Unusable production direction:** important camera / animation instructions are vague or contradictory.
7. **Visual ambiguity:** the main subject or action is obscured or competing focal points make the image unreadable.

## Scorecard (0–5 per category)

| Category | 0 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dramatic intent | No clear purpose | Purpose is present but uneven | Every sequence has a clear audience effect |
| Visual storytelling | Literal / generic illustration | Some images add meaning | Images carry story, subtext, and information deliberately |
| Composition | Unclear hierarchy | Mostly readable | Strong, purposeful, readable staging |
| Camera grammar | Random / repetitive | Generally coherent | Motivated, varied, and precise |
| Blocking / performance | Unclear movement | Readable with gaps | Spatially and emotionally legible |
| Rhythm / edit | Flat or incoherent | Functional pacing | Deliberate variation and strong transitions |
| Continuity | Frequent contradictions | Minor issues | Consistent geography, props, light, and movement |
| Animation feasibility | Not executable | Feasible with clarification | Clear action, timing, and complexity |
| Historical integrity | Unsupported claims | Most details controlled | Evidence boundaries clearly maintained |
| Production handoff | Missing essentials | Mostly usable | Assets, prompts, sound, risks, and timing are actionable |

Maximum: 50.

## Release thresholds

- **45–50:** production-ready, provided no hard-fail remains.
- **38–44:** strong draft; revise weak categories.
- **30–37:** needs structural and visual revision.
- **Below 30:** redesign the board from directorial intent and sequence beats.

A high score never overrides a hard-fail check. Report category scores honestly and list the three highest-impact revisions.

## Anti-AI-slop audit

Ask:
- Could this storyboard belong to almost any unrelated history video?
- Does every shot use a centered subject, shallow depth, dramatic rim light, and slow push-in?
- Does every narration line get an obvious literal picture?
- Are characters posed without intention or relationship?
- Are visual details decorative rather than story-specific?
- Is the scene full of particles, fog, lens flare, or slow motion without physical or dramatic cause?
- Are all shots equally intense, leaving no room for contrast?

If yes, redesign the sequence with more specific evidence, purposeful staging, varied rhythm, and meaningful restraint.
